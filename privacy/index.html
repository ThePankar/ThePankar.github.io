Privacy Policy — Im-off

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Im-off (the "app") is a personal mobile app for tracking paid leave and days off. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it is stored, and who it may be shared with.

In short: your data stays on your phone. The developer runs no server that collects or receives your data. There is no account to create, no profile, and no advertising tracking by the developer. The only data leaving your device is advertising-related (free tier) and whatever you deliberately send to your own Google account.

3. Data handled and where it is stored

The app stores locally on your device (the app's private storage, isolated by Android): your balances and custom counters, the leave you have booked (dates, type, status), and your preferences (country, theme, accrual settings, profile/employment status, reminders). This data never leaves your device unless you enable one of the features below.

The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, or contacts.

4. Optional features that send data off the device

All are off by default and are triggered only by you.

a. Local backup / restore

You can export your data to a .json file that you choose to share or import. The developer has no access to it.

b. Google Cloud (the "Cloud" tier)

If you connect your Google account: Google Drive (drive.file) — the app creates and reads only its own backup file in your Drive; Google Calendar (calendar.app.created) — the app creates a dedicated calendar and its own events. This data goes into your Google account (Google's privacy policy). The developer does not receive it. Access can be revoked at any time from your Google account settings.

c. Contacting the developer

Opens your email app with a pre-filled message; the email is sent only if you send it.

5. Advertising (free tier only)

The free tier shows ads served by Google AdMob, in two formats:

  • a banner at the bottom of the home screen;
  • a full-screen (interstitial) ad, shown at most once per usage session, at a transition point: right after you finish booking a leave entry.

As soon as you buy a paid tier (G-conges+ or Cloud), both formats disappear permanently. The "Support" tip tier keeps advertising.

  • Consent is requested before any advertising, through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), where your country's regulations require it.
  • If you accept: personalised ads are possible, which involves the use of your Android advertising ID and technical data (device type, language, approximate country) by Google.
  • If you decline: non-personalised ads only.
  • Processing is carried out by Google as the advertising provider (Google advertising partners). The developer receives no personal data through advertising and has access only to aggregated, anonymous statistics (impression counts, revenue).

Changing your mind: you can turn off ad personalisation at any time in your device's Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads), or reset your advertising ID. You can also remove advertising entirely by subscribing to a paid tier.

6. In-app purchases

Handled by Google Play Billing. The developer never sees your payment details; Google only reports whether a tier is active, so the corresponding features can be unlocked.

7. What the app does NOT do

  • No account, no sign-up.
  • No server operated by the developer receives your data.
  • No usage analytics and no tracking by the developer.
  • No sale of personal data.

8. Legal bases for processing

For users located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland:

  • App functionality (local data): performance of the service you request by installing and using the app. This data is not transmitted to the developer.
  • Personalised advertising: your consent, collected through the Google UMP form, withdrawable at any time (section 5).
  • Non-personalised advertising: legitimate interest in funding the free version of the app.
  • Google Cloud, backup, reminders: your consent, given by deliberately enabling the feature.

9. International transfers

The developer carries out no transfers. However, the Google services used (AdMob, Play Billing, and Drive/Calendar if you enable them) may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards put in place by Google (standard contractual clauses, applicable adequacy frameworks). See Google's privacy policy.

10. Retention

Your data lives on your device for as long as the app is installed; the developer keeps no copy. To erase everything: uninstall the app, then — if you had enabled Cloud — delete the backup file in your Drive and the dedicated calendar from your Google account. Advertising data is retained by Google according to its own retention periods.

11. Children

The app is aimed at people in employment. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data concerning them. It is not designed for audiences covered by COPPA (United States) or equivalent regimes.

12. Your rights

Because your leave data stays on your device, you retain direct control: you can view, edit, export and delete it from within the app itself, without going through the developer.

European Economic Area (GDPR)

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, as well as the right to withdraw your consent at any time. You may lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority: CNIL (France), Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (Netherlands), your Land authority or the BfDI (Germany), IMY (Sweden).

United Kingdom (UK GDPR / Data Protection Act 2018)

You have equivalent rights and may contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Switzerland (revised FADP / nFADP)

You have, in particular, rights of access and rectification, and may contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

United States — California (CCPA/CPRA) and other states

The developer does not sell your personal information. However, serving personalised ads may qualify as "sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising" under California law. You may opt out at any time by declining ad personalisation (section 5), or by subscribing to a paid tier, which removes all advertising. You also have the rights to know, access, delete and correct, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them.

Exercising your rights

For any request: thepankarapp@gmail.com. As the developer holds no personal data that can identify you, you may be directed to Google for anything concerning advertising or your Google account.

13. Changes

This policy may change; the date at the top indicates the last update.
