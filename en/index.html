What the app does

📊

Balances you can trust

Paid leave and time off, following your own situation: part-time, entry date, accrual period.

🏖️

The best bridge days

The app looks for the most rewarding combinations based on *your* real balance, ranked by return.

✅

Approved or pending

Two separate balances: what is actually deducted, and what would be left if everything gets approved.

⏳

Never lose a day

A reminder before your carry-over deadline, adjustable to your company's rules.

🗓️

A calendar you can read

Your leave, public holidays — national or regional — and weekends, by month or by year.

🔒

Offline, no account

No sign-up, no server. Everything lives on your phone, and nothing leaves it unless you ask.

Backup and calendar, optional. You can connect your Google account to back up to your own Drive and see your leave in Google Calendar. It is entirely optional: without a Google account the app stays fully local.

Find your best bridge days in one tap

The app scans the calendar and spots the most rewarding runs: one day booked next to a public holiday can be worth four days off.

The calculation uses your real balance, the leave you already booked and the holidays of your region — not a generic calendar.

You can also ask the reverse: "I have 5 days to book, where do they buy me the longest break?"

Free bridge-day calendars

Every year the app works out the best runs. The public calendars need no install:

🇫🇷 Ponts 2027 France — 6 days off booked for 18 days of rest. 🇩🇪 Brückentage 2027 Germany — all 16 states compared: 19 free days in Bavaria, 4 in Hamburg. 🇳🇱 Brugdagen 2027 Netherlands — 2 days booked for 8 days off. 🇸🇪 Klämdagar 2027 Sweden — 5 days booked for 16 days off.

Availability

Android app, available in English, French, German, Dutch and Swedish, with the leave rules of each country: France, United Kingdom, Germany, Netherlands, Sweden, Switzerland and the United States.
